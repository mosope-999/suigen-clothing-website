How to measure

  • Chest: around the fullest part, arms relaxed at your sides.
  • Waist: around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hips: around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • Shoulder: from one shoulder edge to the other across your back.

Measure over light clothing and keep the tape snug but not tight. Ask someone to help for the most accurate result.
